Lesson 3

Paragraph Structure and Developing Ideas Clearly

Lesson Goals

Understand complete paragraphs

Write a clear topic sentence

Add relevant supporting sentences

Develop ideas with reasons & examples

Write an effective concluding sentence

Keep one main idea throughout

Avoid repetition and unrelated details

Plan a paragraph through questions

Use the PEEL technique (Writing & Speaking)

Speak clearly about one topic

Part One: Writing Skills

1. What Is a Paragraph?

A paragraph is a group of connected sentences that develop one main idea.

Basic Structure

1. Topic Sentence

Introduces the main idea.

2. Supporting Sentences

Explain, develop, or prove the main idea.

3. Concluding Sentence

Ends the paragraph and returns to the main point.

Example Paragraph

Regular reading can improve a person’s language ability. It introduces readers to new vocabulary and shows them how words are used naturally in context. Reading also exposes learners to different sentence structures and writing styles. For example, a student who reads English articles regularly may begin to recognize useful expressions and use them in personal writing. For these reasons, reading is one of the most effective ways to develop language skills.

Structure Breakdown

  • Topic: Improves language ability.
  • Support 1: Introduces vocabulary.
  • Support 2: Shows language in context.
  • Support 3 (Example): Recognizing & using expressions.
  • Conclusion: Effective way to develop skills.

2. The Topic Sentence

The topic sentence introduces the main idea of the paragraph. It usually tells the reader what the paragraph is about and what the writer wants to say about it.

A strong topic sentence is: Clear, Specific, Focused, and Suitable for development.

Topic and Controlling Idea

"Online education offers students greater flexibility."

  • Topic: online education
  • Controlling idea: greater flexibility

The paragraph should explain how or why online education is flexible.

Weak

"Online education is interesting."

Problem: "Interesting" is too general. The paragraph direction is unclear.

Strong

"Online education allows students to organize their studies around other responsibilities."

This sentence gives the paragraph a clear focus.

2.1 General and Specific Topic Sentences

Too General

"Technology is important."

This topic is too broad for one paragraph.

Better

"Technology has made communication faster and more convenient."

Too Specific

"My brother used a messaging app to contact me at 8:15 last night."

This sounds more like a supporting detail.

Better

"Messaging applications make it easier for families to remain connected across long distances."

2.2 Types of Topic Sentences

Opinion

Working from home can improve employees’ work-life balance.

Cause

Poor time management often leads to unnecessary stress.

Effect

Regular exercise can improve both physical and mental health.

Comparison

Online learning offers more flexibility than traditional classroom learning.

Problem

Excessive social media use can reduce concentration.

Recommendation

Students should develop a regular reading habit.

3. Supporting Sentences

Supporting sentences develop the topic sentence. They may include: Reasons, Explanations, Examples, Facts, Details, Comparisons, Causes, or Effects. A paragraph should not only state an idea; it should explain and support it.

3.1 Giving Reasons

Explains why the main idea is true.

Topic: Time management is an essential skill for students.

"It helps them complete assignments without leaving everything until the final day."

Useful: because, since, one reason is that, this is important because...

3.2 Giving Explanations

Shows how an idea works or what it means.

Point: Time management reduces stress.

"When students divide large tasks into smaller steps, they feel less overwhelmed..."

Useful: this means that, in other words, this happens because, as a result...

3.3 Giving Examples

Examples make ideas clearer and more believable.

Main idea: Technology can support independent learning.

"For example, students can use online videos to review a difficult topic at their own speed."

Useful: for example, for instance, a clear example is, such as...

3.4 Giving Facts or Evidence

Facts and evidence can support formal or academic writing.

"Surveys often show that students value flexibility in online courses."

When using facts, make sure: The information is accurate, the source is reliable, and it directly supports the main idea.

3.5 Showing Cause and Effect

Cause

"Many students delay their work because they do not create a clear schedule."

because, since, due to, as a result of

Effect

"As a result, they may feel stressed before the deadline."

therefore, as a result, consequently, this leads to

4. The Concluding Sentence

The concluding sentence closes the paragraph. It should not introduce a completely new main idea.

Restating Main Idea

Topic: Reading improves language.

"Therefore, regular reading is a valuable part of language learning."

Recommendation

"For these reasons, students should include reading in their weekly study routine."

Showing a Result

"As a result, good time management can improve both performance and well-being."

Weak Conclusion

"I also like watching films."

Problem: The paragraph is about reading; this introduces a new topic.

Strong Conclusion

"Overall, reading regularly can help learners become more confident and independent language users."

5. Paragraph Unity

Unity means that every sentence supports the same main idea. A paragraph should not move between unrelated topics.

Topic: Regular exercise can improve mental health.

  • It can reduce stress.
  • It may improve sleep.
  • Walking can improve mood.
  • Many people prefer online shopping. (Irrelevant)
Weak Paragraph (Lacks Unity)

"Regular exercise can improve mental health. It reduces stress and helps people sleep better. Many sports centres are expensive. Online shopping is becoming more popular. A short walk can improve a person's mood."

Problems: Unrelated sentences, loses focus.

6. Paragraph Development

A paragraph is developed when the reader receives enough information to understand the main idea clearly.

A Weak Paragraph Often Contains:

  • A general statement
  • Repetition
  • No explanation
  • No example
  • No conclusion

A Developed Paragraph Contains:

  • One clear main point
  • Relevant reasons
  • Clear explanations
  • Specific examples
  • A logical ending

Comparison

Weak Paragraph

"Communication is important. It is useful. People communicate every day. It is important at work. Everyone needs communication."

Problems: Repeated words, too general, no explanation/example.

Strong Paragraph

"Effective communication is essential in the workplace because it helps employees understand their responsibilities and cooperate with others. When instructions are clear, workers are less likely to make unnecessary mistakes. For example, a manager who explains a task carefully can prevent confusion and save time. Good communication also allows colleagues to discuss problems before they become more serious. Therefore, strong communication skills can improve both teamwork and productivity."

7. The Question Method for Planning

Before writing, ask questions about the topic to generate ideas, avoid repetition, and organize the paragraph.

Topic: Importance of Time Management

Planning Questions:

  • - What is time management?
  • - Why is it important?
  • - Who needs it?
  • - How does it help?
  • - What problem does it prevent?
  • - What example can I give?
  • - What is my conclusion?

Notes:

organizing time completing responsibilities reducing stress student preparing for exam
Final Paragraph

"Time management is an important skill because it helps people organize their responsibilities more effectively. When individuals plan their time, they can divide large tasks into smaller and more manageable steps. This prevents them from leaving everything until the last moment. For example, a student who begins preparing for an exam several weeks in advance is likely to feel less stressed than a student who studies only the night before. Therefore, effective time management can improve performance and reduce unnecessary pressure."

8. Building a Paragraph Step by Step

  • 1. Main Point: Learning a foreign language creates new opportunities.
  • 2. Add Reason: ...because it allows people to communicate with a wider range of individuals.
  • 3. Add Explanation: This can be useful in education, travel, and professional life.
  • 4. Add Example: For example, a person who speaks English may be able to apply for international jobs...
  • 5. Add Conclusion: For these reasons, language learning can have a positive effect on personal and professional development.

9. Weak and Strong Paragraph Comparison

Topic: The benefits of online learning
Weak Paragraph

"Online learning is good. It is flexible. Students study at home. It saves time. It has many benefits. I like online learning."

Problems: Topic sentence too general. "Good" is vague. Short/repetitive. No explanation. No example. Final sentence too personal.
Strong Paragraph

"Online learning offers students greater flexibility because they can study from different locations and organize lessons around their responsibilities. It can also save commuting time and make education more accessible to people who live far from schools. For example, a working student may attend an evening lesson from home without travelling across the city. Although online learning requires self-discipline, it can be highly effective. Overall, online education can provide a practical learning option for busy routines."

Why it is better: Focused topic sentence. Clear reasons & explanations. Specific example. Balanced point. Clear conclusion.

10. Paragraph Order

Supporting ideas should follow a logical order.

General to Specific

"Exercise improves overall health. It strengthens the body, supports the heart, and can reduce stress. For example, regular walking may improve energy and mood."

Problem to Solution

"Many students struggle to complete assignments on time. One solution is to create a weekly schedule and divide large tasks into smaller steps."

Cause to Effect

"Some people use their phones late at night. As a result, they may sleep less and feel tired the next day."

11. Basic Paragraph Linking

Addition

also, in addition, furthermore

Reason

because, since, the main reason is

Example

for example, for instance

Result

therefore, as a result

Contrast

however, although, on the other hand

Conclusion

overall, in conclusion

Important Note: Do not begin every sentence with a linking word. Use them only when they make the relationship between ideas clearer.

12. Common Paragraph Problems

12.1 No Clear Topic Sentence

People use technology every day. Some use phones.

Technology has become an essential part of everyday communication.

12.2 Topic Sentence Too Broad

Life is difficult.

Managing work and personal responsibilities can be challenging.

12.3 Repetition

Reading is important because reading helps vocabulary. Reading also helps grammar.

Reading is valuable because it develops vocabulary and natural grammar patterns.

12.4 Unrelated Details

Public transportation reduces traffic... My brother bought a new car last week. Trains are useful.

The sentence about the brother's car breaks unity.

12.5 Unsupported Opinion

Online learning is better.

Online learning can be more convenient for working adults because...

12.6 Weak Example

Exercise is useful. For example, many people exercise.

Exercise can reduce stress. For example, a 30-minute walk after work...

12.7 New Idea in Conclusion

For these reasons, reading is important. I also enjoy travelling.

The final sentence introduces a different topic.

12.8 Paragraph Too Short

Time management is important. It helps people. Students need it.

Grammatically correct, but underdeveloped. Needs explanation and example.

Practice Exercises

Practice 1: Identify Paragraph Parts

"Regular exercise can improve a person’s daily energy level. It strengthens the body and helps people become more physically active. Exercise may also improve sleep, which allows the body to recover more effectively. For example, a person who walks regularly may feel less tired while completing everyday tasks. Therefore, physical activity can help people feel healthier and more energetic."

Topic: Regular exercise can improve a person's daily energy level.

Reason/Expl: Strengthens body, improves sleep/recovery.

Example: Person walking regularly feels less tired.

Conclusion: Therefore, physical activity helps people feel healthier.

Practice 2: Improve Topic Sentences

  • 1. Technology is good. → Tech makes communication faster.
  • 2. Reading is important. → Reading improves vocabulary.
  • 3. Social media has effects. → Excessive use affects concentration.
  • 4. Exercise helps people. → Exercise improves mental health.

Practice 3: Remove Irrelevant Sentence

"Learning English can improve professional opportunities. Many companies communicate with international customers. English can help employees participate in global projects. My favourite colour is blue. Therefore, English may be valuable in the workplace."

Irrelevant: My favourite colour is blue.

Part Two: Speaking Skills

13. Speaking in Organized Units

A strong spoken answer is similar to a paragraph. It normally contains: 1. One main point, 2. An explanation, 3. An example, 4. A final connection to the question.

Weak Spoken Answer

Q: Why is exercise important?

"Exercise is important. It is healthy. People should exercise. It is good for everyone."

Problems: Idea repeated, not explained, no example.

Strong Spoken Answer (Spoken Paragraph)

"Exercise is important because it supports both physical and mental health. It can strengthen the body, reduce stress, and improve sleep. For example, a short daily walk may help a person feel calmer and more energetic. Therefore, regular exercise can improve a person’s overall quality of life."

14. The PEEL Technique

PEEL is useful for: Longer answers, Opinion questions, Presentations, and Explaining ideas clearly.

P - Point

State main idea.
Reading is one of the best ways to improve vocabulary.

E - Explanation

Explain how/why.
It allows learners to see new words in meaningful sentences.

E - Example

Support explanation.
For example, noticing common expressions in articles.

L - Link

Connect back.
Therefore, reading makes vocabulary learning effective.

15. PEEL and PREP

PEEL and PREP are similar, but their focus is slightly different.

TechniqueStructureBest use
PREPPoint, Reason, Example, PointClear opinions and persuasion
PEELPoint, Explanation, Example, LinkDeveloping and explaining one idea

PREP Focus

"I believe reading is essential because it improves vocabulary. For example, learners meet new words in context. Therefore, reading should be part of every language-learning plan."

PEEL Focus

"Reading is an effective way to improve vocabulary. It introduces learners to new words in meaningful contexts, which makes their meanings easier to understand. For example... Therefore, reading helps learners build vocabulary naturally."

PEEL usually gives more attention to explanation.

16. Weak and Strong PEEL Comparison

Q: Why is communication important at work?

"Communication is important because people need to communicate at work."

Problems: Repeats question, no explanation, no example.

Strong PEEL Answer

"Communication is important at work because it helps employees understand responsibilities and cooperate effectively. Clear communication reduces confusion and allows problems to be solved more quickly. For example, if a manager explains a task clearly, employees are less likely to make mistakes. Therefore, good communication can improve both teamwork and productivity."

17. Planning a PEEL Answer

Q: Why is time management important?

  • - organize responsibilities
  • - avoid last-minute work
  • - smaller tasks
  • - student preparing for exam
  • - reduce stress

"[P] Time management is important because it helps people organize responsibilities and avoid last-minute work. [E] When large tasks are divided into smaller steps, they become easier to complete. [E] For example, a student who begins preparing for an exam several weeks early is likely to feel less stressed. [L] Therefore, effective time management can improve both performance and confidence."

18. Extending One Main Point

Do not add many weak points. It is better to explain one strong point fully.

Weak (Many points)

"Technology is useful because it is fast, interesting, modern, easy, important, and students like it."

Strong (One developed point)

"Technology can make learning more accessible. Students can reach lessons... from different locations. For example, a person who lives far from a university may still attend an online course. Therefore..."

19. Staying Relevant

Every sentence should support your main point.

Q: Why is reading useful? (Irrelevant details)

"Reading improves vocabulary. I usually read in the evening. My sister prefers films. Yesterday, I bought a new phone."

Relevant Answer

"Reading improves vocabulary because learners see words in meaningful contexts. For example... Therefore, regular reading can support more accurate language use."

20. Useful PEEL Language

Point

  • One important point is…
  • I believe that…
  • A major benefit is…
  • The main problem is…

Explanation

  • This is important because…
  • This means that…
  • This allows people to…
  • The reason is that…

Example

  • For example…
  • For instance…
  • A clear example is…
  • This can be seen when…

Link

  • Therefore…
  • For this reason…
  • Overall…
  • This shows that…

21. Speaking for 1-2 Minutes

To speak for longer:

  1. State the point
  2. Explain the point
  3. Add a specific example
  4. Add a result if useful
  5. Link back to the question

"One useful habit for students is planning their week in advance. This helps them see which tasks are urgent... For example, a student can decide which days to use for reading... A weekly plan can also reduce stress... Therefore, planning ahead helps students become organized."

22. Avoiding Repetition

Repetitive "Reading is important because reading improves vocabulary, and reading gives knowledge, so reading is important."
Improved "Reading is important because it improves vocabulary and increases general knowledge. It also exposes learners to different ideas... For these reasons, regular reading can support language development."

Use: Pronouns, Synonyms, Related expressions, Different sentence structures.

23. Common Speaking Problems

  • Repeating the same sentence
  • Giving a point without explanation
  • Giving an example unrelated to the point
  • Changing the topic
  • Listing many ideas without development
  • Speaking from a complete script
  • Using examples before introducing main point
  • Ending suddenly
  • Using linking phrases without meaning
  • Speaking long without answering question

24. Speaking Self-Assessment

  • Did I state one clear main point?
  • Did I explain what it means and why it's important?
  • Did I give a relevant example?
  • Did I link the answer back to the question?
  • Did I stay focused and avoid repetition?
  • Did I use keywords instead of a full script?
  • Did I use PEEL correctly and end naturally?

25. Writing Self-Editing Checklist

Topic Sentence

  • Is the main idea clear?
  • Is topic narrow enough?
  • Does it show direction?

Support & Conclusion

  • Does every sentence support topic?
  • Included reason/expl/example?
  • Does conclusion return to main idea without new topic?

Development

  • Is paragraph detailed enough?
  • Avoided repetition?
  • Logical order & specific examples?
